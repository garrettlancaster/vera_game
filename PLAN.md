This will be an educational game that hides math instruction as a necessary part of game play. 

The game will track the player's progress and increase or decrease difficulty as the player progresses.

Difficulty should follow a natural hierarchy of understanding, with an emphasis on number sense and intuitive understanding rather than rote memorization.

AI players should provide help and instructional feedback in a friendly way. AI characters might also provide indirect help by being observable solving problems in the game.
